# Auto Dark Theme Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Auto Dark Theme is a Chrome extension that intelligently converts light-themed websites to a dark theme.

## 1. Local Processing
All color detection, CSS injection, and theme conversions happen entirely within your browser on your device.

## 2. No Data Transmission
Auto Dark Theme does not collect, store, or transmit any of your browsing history, page content, or personal information. 

## 3. Settings and Storage
The extension uses `chrome.storage.sync` to save your preferences (such as enabling/disabling the theme or managing your whitelist) across your devices. These settings are private to your Chrome account and are not accessible by us.
