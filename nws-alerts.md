# NWS Alerts Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

NWS Alerts provides real-time weather notifications. We value your privacy and transparency.

## 1. Data Processing
- **API Usage:** The application polls the public National Weather Service (NWS) API to retrieve weather alerts. No personal identifiers are sent during these requests.
- **Local Filtering:** Your selected counties and severity levels are stored locally on your device and are used only to filter the data received from the API.

## 2. No Data Collection
We do not track your location via GPS. Location settings are manually entered by the user and remain on the local device.
