# Social-Post-Scheduler Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Social-Post-Scheduler allows you to post to Facebook and Twitter on a weekly schedule.

## 1. Authentication and Access
The application uses OAuth to authenticate with Facebook and Twitter. We do not see or store your login credentials. Access tokens granted by these services are used solely to perform the scheduling actions you authorize.

## 2. Data Usage
The application only accesses data necessary to facilitate scheduled posting. This includes post content and scheduling preferences. We do not sell, rent, or share your social media data with third parties.

## 3. Local Storage
Configuration and scheduling data are stored locally or in your private environment.
