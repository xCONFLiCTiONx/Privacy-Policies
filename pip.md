# Picture-in-Picture Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

Picture-in-Picture is a utility extension for video players.

## 1. Functionality
The extension interacts with video elements on webpages to enable the native browser PiP mode. It does not monitor video content or track viewing habits.

## 2. Permissions
Requires access to active tabs only to trigger the PiP functionality on your command.
