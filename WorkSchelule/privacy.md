# WorkSchelule Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

WorkSchelule is an Android application designed to manage work shifts and alarms.

## 1. Calendar Integration
WorkSchelule uses a user-provided ICS (iCalendar) link to fetch shift information. This link is stored locally on your device. The app fetches data from this link solely to populate your shift schedule and set corresponding alarms.

## 2. Permissions
- **Notifications:** Used to provide alerts for upcoming shifts and active alarms.
- **Alarms:** Requires permission to schedule exact alarms to ensure you are notified reliably.
- **Overlay:** Used to display the alarm screen over other apps for immediate visibility.

## 3. Data Privacy
All shift data and settings are stored locally on your device. WorkSchelule does not transmit your schedule, location, or personal details to any external server.
