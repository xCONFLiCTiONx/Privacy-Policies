# StickyPad Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

StickyPad is a Windows desktop application for keeping notes handy.

## 1. Data Storage
All notes and content created within StickyPad are stored locally on your device.

## 2. No Data Collection
StickyPad does not upload your notes to any cloud service, nor does it collect or transmit any analytics or usage data. Your notes remain private and under your control.
